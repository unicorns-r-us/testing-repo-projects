# The Unicorns' Bright Parade

When morning paints the hills in gold  
And dew makes every meadow shine,  
A silver hoofbeat skips the road—  
The unicorns are right on time!

**Chorus**  
Oh, unicorns, you light the sky,  
With rainbow manes that stream and fly.  
Brave hearts, bright horns, and magic grace—  
You make the world a wondrous place!

They leap above the babbling brook,  
They race the clouds across the blue.  
With every kind and joyful look,  
They make old dreams feel bright and new.

**Chorus**  
Oh, unicorns, you light the sky,  
With rainbow manes that stream and fly.  
Brave hearts, bright horns, and magic grace—  
You make the world a wondrous place!

So let the hills all sing along,  
Let every star join in the view:  
The world feels brighter, bold, and strong  
With awesome unicorns like you!
