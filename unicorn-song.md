# The Unicorn Parade

When morning paints the hills in gold,  
And dew shines bright on every fern,  
A silver hoofbeat skips the path—  
The unicorns are here to turn!

**Chorus**  
Oh, unicorns, so bold and bright,  
You fill the day with colors light!  
With flowing manes and hearts so kind,  
You leave a little wonder behind.

They race beneath the rainbow sky,  
Their laughter ringing through the air.  
They lend a hand, they share their joy,  
And make the world a brighter place to share.
